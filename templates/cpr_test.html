CPR Test RTT v0.0 · read-only, places no orders

Runs on every closed paper trade in your history. For each trade, the CPR of its day is worked out from the previous day's high, low and close (Pivot = (H+L+C)/3, BC = (H+L)/2, TC = 2×Pivot − BC). Narrow = the CPR width is among the narrowest 25% of the previous 20 days; Wide = the widest 25%. Aligned = a Buy that opened above the CPR or a Sell that opened below it.

P&L is each trade's own paper P&L %. "Day move" and "Best move" are the stock's, from the day's open in the trade's direction. Small groups prove nothing, so look at the count (n) first. Your trades come from Chartink alerts only, and RTT holds one trade at a time, so this is "how your trades behaved", not a general rule. Results are held in memory and recalculated each time you run the test.
