Covers stocks with a Buy or Sell alert on the chosen day. 9:15 ratio = first 5-min candle volume ÷ its average over the previous 10 sessions. Day vol = whole-day volume so far ÷ the average at the same clock time. Below 20d high = previous close vs the highest high of the last 20 sessions. Percent moves are from the previous close. A day still in progress shows partial numbers. Click a column heading to sort.
