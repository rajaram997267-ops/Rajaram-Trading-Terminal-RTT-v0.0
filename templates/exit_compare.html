How to read it: every row of the Result table uses the same trades (when the box above is ticked), so the numbers are directly comparable. "Best on" counts the trades where that rule had the highest % (ties count for each). "What actually ran" is what your trades really made, which mixes in whatever strategy was active on each day. Approximations: each exit is priced at the option's 1-minute close at the signal time, with no slippage or charges. FIB touches use the underlying's 1-minute highs/lows. The RSI/EMA settings used are the ones currently in Settings, not necessarily those active on the day. Trades whose option contract has expired may have no price data and are listed as "no data" in the count above. A rule needs roughly 30 or more trades before its numbers mean much.
