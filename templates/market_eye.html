Sector Performance at 09:25 AM

Average % change of the sector's F&O stocks at 09:25 IST against the previous close. Fixed for the day once calculated.
SectorStocks% at 09:25Est. net flow (₹ cr)Flow %Buy / Sell stocks
Estimated flow = the first two candles (09:15–09:25) of each F&O stock, volume split into buying and selling by where each candle closes in its range, in ₹. Net flow = buying minus selling; Flow % = net ÷ total. Buy / Sell stocks = how many stocks had more estimated buying than selling. It is an estimate from price and volume, not real institutional orders.

Change in Open Interest (OI)

Nearest stock future of each stock that had a Buy or Sell alert on the date. Today = live from Upstox; earlier days = the ~09:26 snapshot logged each morning (it cannot be backfilled, so days before logging started stay empty). Same idea as NSE's OI Spurts page, built from Upstox data because NSE blocks cloud servers.
