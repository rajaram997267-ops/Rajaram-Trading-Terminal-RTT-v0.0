What the families mean

Time hold - sell after a fixed number of minutes (or hold to the close).
Stop only / Stop + target - fixed protection (and profit-taking) measured from the alert price.
Trailing stop - sell when the option falls X% from its highest price since the alert (optionally only after it is up +5%).
Breakeven + trail - once up +5/+8%, the stop can never drop below the entry price; then it trails.
Volume exit - your own idea: sell when the stock's directional volume fades (turns Weak, drops below Strong, drops below Strong twice running, or halves vs entry), checked at each 5-minute close.
Volume + trail - whichever comes first: volume fades or the price trail is hit.
Volume-adaptive - one trailing stop whose width follows the live volume: wide while volume is Strong, tighter as it turns Moderate, and out (or very tight) when it turns Weak; or the width steps down a ratio ladder (e.g. 15% at 3x+, 10% at 1.5x+, 6% at 1x+, exit below 1x). The hard stop stays throughout.
RSI - your current RSI Simple and RSI Momentum rules (today's Settings), as the benchmark to beat.
Scale-out - book half at a target, let the rest ride a trail or a volume exit.
