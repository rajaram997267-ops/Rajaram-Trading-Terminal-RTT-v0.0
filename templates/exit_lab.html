For every closed trade that came from a logged alert: what you actually made vs what RSI Simple Exit would have made on the same option price path (gross, before costs; P&L = quantity × (exit - alert price)).
1st 5 Min FIB Exit on your closed-trade history
For every closed trade that came from a logged alert: what you actually made vs what the 1st 5 Min FIB levels strategy would have made (gross, before costs), with RSI Simple alongside for comparison. FIB trades on the stock's own price, so this downloads each day's stock candles from Upstox - the first time it can take a minute and refreshes itself. Needs a valid Upstox login.
