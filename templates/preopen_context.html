Sector rotation (RRG)

Each sector against the F&O market. Leading = strong and strengthening, Weakening = strong but fading, Improving = weak but picking up, Lagging = weak and weakening. Simplified: RS-Ratio is the sector/market ratio against its 50-day average; Momentum is its change over 10 days. The tail shows the last 12 days.
