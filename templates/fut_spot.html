Today's alerts only. For each stock: the nearest-expiry future and the spot (cash) price, each with its % change vs the previous close. Premium = how far the future trades above spot. Lead = future % minus spot % in the alert's own direction (for a Sell, the future falling more than spot is a positive lead). Refreshes every ~20 s while the Dashboard or this page is open.

Futures almost always trade above spot because of the cost of carry (interest until expiry, less dividends), so a positive premium alone is normal and the premium shrinks as expiry nears. The more useful reading is whether the future is moving MORE than spot (Lead). Whether that predicts continuation is unproven: check it against your own alerts over a few weeks before using it for entries. On expiry day the nearest future expires today.
